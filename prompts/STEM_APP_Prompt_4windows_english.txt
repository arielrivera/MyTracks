1. General objective
Limbus Spjlit Pro will be a native Windows application to separate a musical mix into tracks
independent through local artificial intelligence models.
It should be aimed at musicians, producers, students and users without technical knowledge. The main flow
will be: load a song, choose what to separate, choose a folder, process, listen to the tracks and export.
Priorities, in this order:

1. Real quality of separation.

2. Exact synchronization of playback and export.

3. Stability and absence of false silent files.

4. Ease of use.

5. Privacy and local processing.

6. Compatibility with Windows 10 and Windows 11.

7. Portability of the installer.

8. Correct licenses and attributions.

9. Minimalist, beautiful and worked design.
Don't build a website, PWA, or Electron app.
Develop a native Windows application. Prefer Cif with a current LTS version of .NET, WPF, and pattern
MVVM for its maturity, stability and direct access to the audio system. You can use WinUI 3 only if
you verify that packaging, multitrack playback, and compatibility with target versions are the same
of reliable.
Before pinning versions, check the current LTS version of .NET and supported versions of Windows App
SDK, WPF, NAudio and the other dependencies. Records all versions in a reproducible way.
