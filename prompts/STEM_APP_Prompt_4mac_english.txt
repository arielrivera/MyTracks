Acts as a software architect, senior macOS developer, digital audio specialist, machine
learning applied to musical separation, product design and application packaging.
I want you to design, program, test and package a macOS desktop application from scratch
called Ariel's Splitter.
Work autonomously until you obtain a functional and verifiable application. Don't just describe code:
inspects the environment, investigates, deploys, compiles, runs actual tests, and generates the final artifacts.
If you encounter a technical or legal impediment, don't hide it or pretend that a feature exists. Continue with everything
what can be completed and explains exactly what model, license, certificate or resource is missing.
1. General objective
Ariel's Splitter will be a native application to separate a musical mix into independent tracks using
local artificial intelligence models.
It should be designed for musicians, producers, students and users without technical knowledge. The flow must be
direct: load a song, choose what to separate, choose a folder, process, listen to the tracks and export.
Priorities, in this order:

1. Real quality of separation.

2. Exact synchronization of playback and export.

3. Stability and absence of false silent files.

4. Ease of use.

5. Privacy and local processing.

6. Installer portability.

7. Correct licenses and attributions.

8. Minimalist, beautiful and worked design.
Do not build a website or an Electron application. Develop a native macOS application,
preferably with Swift, SwiftUI, AppKit, AVFoundation and AVAudioEngine.
2. Name, identity and design
Product name: Ariel's Splitter.
The design must be:

* Minimalist.

* Clean.

* Elegant.

* Professional.

* Friendly.

* With visual personality.

5. Other Logic
Plugin logic is essential.
* If the user selects only “Voices”, exports “Voices.wav” and “Other.wav”.
* In that case, Other must contain everything that is not voice.
* If you select “Battery” only, export “Full Battery.wav”, its details where applicable, and
“Other.wav”.
* In that case, Other must contain everything that is not a battery.
* If you select multiple instruments, Other should contain everything that has not been selected.
* If you select all available categories, generates Other only if there is residual information that does not
reliably belongs to any track.
* Other must not be an artificially created silent file.
* The sum of the main and Other tracks should reconstruct the mix with as little error as possible.
* Avoid duplicating material when exporting a master track and its subtracks simultaneously.
For detailed voices:
*Lead vocals and backing vocals must come from a real vocal separation.
* Vocal effects must not be a copy of the voice.
*Noise must be a real independent track.
*If a separation depends on first obtaining the full voice, execute that stage internally even if the
user has not checked “Voices”.

11. Real-time behavior
* Pressing mute should not stop playback.
* Pressing alone should not stop playback.
* Changing the volume should not stop playback.
* None of these operations should return the cursor to the beginning.
* Seek during playback should continue from the new point.
* The seek should be smooth, without bounce, double attack, clicks or noticeable silences.
* Implement a short crossfade or gain ramps when seeking.
*Do not reset the entire engine to change mute, solo or volume.
* Play after pause should continue from current position.
* The meaning of Stop and "return to the beginning" must be explicit.
